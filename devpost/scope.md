# Level Up — Working Title

A gamified to-do list where positive real-life activities earn progress and cosmetics for a personalized cartoon character.

## The Unique Kernel
Everyday effort visibly develops your character: completing quests earns XP and coins, levels unlock access to items, and coins buy cosmetics you can equip. The connected rewards make progress tangible and give players something to work toward.

## Who It's For
Someone who enjoys games but struggles to start everyday chores or positive activities, and sometimes stays at home doing nothing instead. They want an appealing reason to take action and see their effort accumulate.

## The Core Loop
Open the quest catalog, add activities to daily quests, do them in real life, then check them off to receive XP and coins. Level up, unlock items, buy cosmetics, and equip them on your character. Return to choose more quests and develop the character further.

Completion is based on honesty. Completed quests leave the active list and can be added again manually when needed. Daily quests persist until completed; they do not reset each day or automatically recur.

## Inspiration & Identity
The learner's attached Canva mockup, titled “Personal Growth,” is the visual reference; no public reference URL was supplied. It shows a friendly full-body 3D cartoon human, rounded gray dashboard panels, and bright green progress accents. The character can resemble the player or act as their personal companion. Everyone starts in a plain white tank top and gray pants, with a few initial appearance choices; earned cosmetics provide later personalization.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner sometimes lacks motivation to do everyday things. They want to make positive actions fun by giving them visible rewards and character progression.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In the learner's words: a person opens the quest catalog, adds daily quests, completes the tasks in real life, checks them off, and receives XP and coins that let them upgrade their character and get better clothes or cosmetics.

The demonstrable moment is completing a quest, seeing rewards and progress update, then purchasing and equipping an unlocked cosmetic that visibly changes the character. Saved progress persists between visits. The hackathon targets a small proof of concept in roughly 2–4 hours of active work, demonstrated in a short video with a public GitHub repository; deployment is optional.

## The POC Boundary
- One personal dashboard with the player's name and a cartoon human, a few initial customization choices, and white tank tops and gray starting pants.
- A small catalog of home, health, and social positivity quests.
- A daily quest list that retains unfinished tasks until completion.
- Quest completion awards XP and coins, removes the quest, and advances level progress.
- A small cosmetic shop: coins purchase items, some items require a certain level, and equipped items visibly change the character.
- Saved character, quest, and reward progress between visits.

Exact reward amounts, level thresholds, customization options, cosmetic presentation, and save behavior will be defined in the PRD and technical spec.

## Later
- Favorites for quickly finding frequently used catalog quests.
- An ongoing quest log separate from dashboard daily quests.
- Detailed personal skill stats.
- Achievements and milestone reward chests containing random cosmetics. The broader vision includes a large collection, rare duplicates, and potentially converting duplicates into coins.
- Larger quest and cosmetic catalogs.
- Households or parties that make contributions visible, plus possible leaderboards. Shared contribution recognition is more central to this idea than competition.

## Explicitly Cut
- Creature transformations are set aside because the learner worries they could look less appealing than the human character and are not necessary to the current reward loop. They may be reconsidered later.
- Automatic recurring quests and daily resets are excluded: the need to repeat a task depends on real life, and unfinished quests should stay until completed.
